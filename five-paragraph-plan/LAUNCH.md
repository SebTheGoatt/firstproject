# Operation First Formation: launch order for The Five-Paragraph Plan

Issued 26 OCT 2026. A separate product from Post One Lawn Co.: Post One's "no military jargon" rule covers lawn
customer copy. This product is built on the military format on purpose.

## 1. Situation

**Ground truth.** The product is the **Field Kit, $30 one-time**: the full planner (all five paragraphs, weekly review,
pocket card; a file buyers keep, works offline) plus the 28-page printable Field Notebook PDF with four finished
orders. The public page is the storefront: paragraphs 1 and 2 free, then "Finish your order · $30". It costs $0 to run
(no server, no accounts, no data collected). After checkout fees about $25 of each $30 sale is ours (Gumroad: 10% +
$0.50, plus card processing; check their pricing page when listing).

**Working against us.**
- Self-help is crowded. Habit apps, planners and coaches all promise the same thing.
- Most free tools get used once and forgotten.
- Civilians don't know what an OPORD is.

**On our side.**
- A format millions of service members and veterans already know and trust. Nobody owns it in self-help yet.
- The founder is a real 0311/8156 veteran, former high school teacher, MBA, and dad. Each of those is a way in.
- The output is shareable by design ("Operation Clean Slate" on a lock screen gets asked about).

**Time and terrain.** A few hours a week, phone-first. New Year's goal season (Dec 26 – Jan 31) is the biggest
window of the year. Veterans Day (Nov 11) is 16 days out.

## 2. Mission

We sell 100 Field Kits (about $2,500 after fees) by 31 JAN 2027, using only free channels, so that we prove people
will pay before spending a dollar on ads or anything else.

## 3. Execution

**Intent.** Paying buyers who finish their order and tell someone, plus real proof of which audience buys most
(veterans, parents, students, or teams). The free paragraphs 1 and 2 do the selling: every post sends people there.

**Phase 1 (Oct 27 – Nov 11): First formation.**
- Share the page's link publicly (Share menu on the artifact) or host the file free on GitHub Pages / Netlify.
- Write your own real order first. Post it (blurred where personal) as proof you use it.
- Veterans Day post on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram: "The Marine Corps taught me one way to plan. I use it for
  my family now. Here it is, free."
- Share in r/USMC, r/Veterans, r/militaryfamily (read each sub's self-promo rules first; lead with the story, link in
  a comment if required).

**Phase 2 (Nov 12 – Dec 25): Build the formation.**
- 3 short videos a week (30–45 s, phone camera): one paragraph per video, one real example each. "Paragraph 4 is
  why your diet fails" will outperform "check out my app".
- Teacher lane: a one-page classroom version (Situation = where my grade is, Mission = what I'll raise it to). Offer
  it free to the teachers you know and to JROTC instructors. One teacher reaches 150 students a year.
- Veteran organizations: email Team Rubicon, Team RWB, local VFW and American Legion posts and the base transition
  office. Offer a free 30-minute "plan your next mission" session (Zoom or in person).
- Ask everyone who finishes: "Who's your second in command? Send them this." The tool already asks for one.

**Phase 3 (Dec 26 – Jan 31): New Year's offensive.**
- "Don't make a resolution. Write an order." One post every day for 14 days, one real example each day.
- Collect results with permission only: "What was your mission, and how's week 3 going?"

**Daily and weekly tasks.**
- Daily: one post or video, reply to every comment.
- Weekly (Sunday, 20 min): after action review on the numbers below.

**If–then.**
- If a channel brings nothing after 3 weeks, then stop it and double the one that works.
- If one audience clearly wins (say, parents), then make the examples and posts for them.
- If someone shares something heavy (crisis, self-harm), then point them to 988 (press 1 for veterans). We are a
  planning tool, not counselors.
