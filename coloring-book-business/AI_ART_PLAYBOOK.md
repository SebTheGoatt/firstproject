# AI Art Playbook for Coloring Pages

## Rules that protect the business
1. **Original characters only.** Never put Bluey, Mickey, SpongeBob or any
   other brand or character in a prompt, a title, a tag or a file name.
2. **Know the policy of each platform.** Amazon KDP requires you to disclose
   AI-generated content when you publish. Check each platform's current
   terms before uploading.
3. **Copyright caution.** In the US, purely AI-generated images may not get
   copyright protection. Add real human authorship: your character designs,
   hand edits, original layouts, titles and text. Keep your sketches and
   working files as proof.
4. **Check the tool's commercial-use terms** for the free tool you choose.
5. **Quality-check every page.** No stray text, extra fingers, closed-off
   areas that can't be colored, or tiny details a toddler can't handle.

## Character sheets
Write each character once and paste the same description into every prompt.

> **Pip the Pup**: a round, friendly puppy with big floppy ears, a tiny
> round nose, a short tail and a small scarf. Simple shapes, thick outlines.

Keep each sheet in a file so characters stay consistent across 100+ pages.

## Prompt template
```
Children's coloring page, black and white line art only, thick clean
outlines, no shading, no grey, no filled black areas, white background,
large simple shapes, easy for ages 2-5 to color.
Subject: [character sheet text] [doing activity] in [setting].
Style: friendly cartoon, rounded shapes, cheerful.
```

## Page ideas (per 25-page pack)
- 5 hero pages (character close-ups)
- 8 activity pages (park, bath time, birthday, picnic)
- 5 learning pages (count the balloons, shapes, ABC corner)
- 4 scene pages (full-page, still simple)
- 3 bonus pages (certificate, mini maze, dot-to-dot)

## Cleanup workflow (in Canva free or any editor)
1. Upscale to print size (8.5 x 11 in at 300 DPI if the tool allows it).
2. Set the threshold so lines are pure black on pure white.
3. Close gaps and remove specks.
4. Add a small brand footer and page number.
5. Export all pages as one PDF.

## Packaging checklist
- Cover page with the product title and character lineup
- "How to use" page for parents (print at 100%, US Letter)
- Pages in a consistent order
- A thank-you page with a review request and the Coloring Club link
