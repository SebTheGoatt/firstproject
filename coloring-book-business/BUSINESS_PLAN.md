# Kids Coloring Book Business: Original Characters, $0 Startup

Built for: a solo founder, 10+ hrs/week, no audience, faceless marketing,
AI-assisted art, a laptop and a phone.

## 1. Honest framing (read first)

- **$0 is achievable for startup cost.** Everything below uses free tiers or
  pay-only-when-you-sell platforms. The real cost is your time.
- **$4k/month is a stretch goal, not a promise.** Most solo digital-product
  shops take 4-9 months to get there, and some never do. The plan below is
  built to give it the best shot. Check the numbers at each milestone and
  adjust if they lag.
- **No Bluey, Mickey Mouse Clubhouse or SpongeBob.** Those characters are
  trademarked and copyrighted. Pages of them get listings removed and shops
  banned. We use **original characters in the same warm, silly, kid-friendly
  vibe** instead. That also means you own a brand you can grow.

## 2. The product

**Brand (placeholder, rename freely):** *Wiggle Wood Coloring Club*

**Characters (original; designed by you, so keep a character sheet):**
| Character | Hook | Kid appeal |
|---|---|---|
| Pip the Pup + family | Cozy family adventures at home and the park | Relatable, like family-cartoon shows |
| Captain Bubbles & the Reef Crew | Goofy sea creatures with a boat | Silly humor, ocean pages |
| The Clubhouse Critters | Friends who solve a tiny problem each page | Counting, shapes, letters (educational) |

**Page style:** thick clean outlines, big shapes, little detail. Ages 2-6 is the
sweet spot, and it fits your own 2-year-old as a built-in tester.

**Product ladder (this is how it reaches $4k):**
| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Free lead magnet | $0 | 5-page sampler PDF in exchange for an email |
| Mini pack | $4.99 | 25 pages, one theme (a Pip's Day pack, a Reef Crew pack, etc.) |
| Mega bundle | $14.99 | 150+ pages, every theme |
| Coloring Club | $7/mo | New pages every week, a monthly activity pack, and a printable certificate |
| Paperback (KDP) | ~$7.99 | Same pages as a print-on-demand book, no upfront cost |

Why a membership: one-off sales reset to zero each month. A club stacks.

## 4. The $0 stack

| Job | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Sell digital files | Gumroad or Payhip (free plan) | 0 upfront; they take a cut per sale |
| Print books | Amazon KDP | 0 upfront; royalty per sale |
| Email list | MailerLite or Kit (free tier) | $0 under the subscriber cap |
| Landing page | GitHub Pages (this repo) | $0 |
| Page layout / PDFs | Canva free, or Google Slides to PDF | $0 |
| Art | Free AI image tools + your cleanup (see `AI_ART_PLAYBOOK.md`) | $0 |
| Social | Pinterest, TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts | $0 |

Skip Etsy at first. It's a great marketplace but charges $0.20 per listing, so
it isn't strictly $0. Add it once revenue covers it.

## 5. Marketing (faceless, no audience needed)

**Principle:** parents of toddlers search Pinterest and Google for "free
printable coloring pages". Find them there, give value, collect emails, sell.

1. **Pinterest is your #1 engine.** Free, evergreen, built for faceless
   printables. Pin 5-10 pins/day (batch-create weekly). Keyword-rich titles
   like "Free printable ocean coloring pages for toddlers". Each pin links to
   a free sampler, which feeds your email list.
2. **Faceless short video.** Three formats, all filmable from your phone with
   no face or voice:
   - Timelapse of a page being colored.
   - Flip-through of a pack ("25 pages for $4.99").
   - "Pages I made this week" slideshow with trending audio.
3. **Free sampler to email.** The 5-page sampler is the lead magnet. A 4-email
   welcome sequence (value, character story, mini-pack offer, club offer)
   converts a share of subscribers into buyers on autopilot.
4. **Parents' communities.** Join, answer questions, and share the free sampler
   only where self-promotion is allowed. Be helpful first.
5. **Teachers.** Add a "classroom license" bundle ($19.99). Teachers buy in bulk
   and talk to each other.
6. **Seasonal drops.** Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and Valentine's
   packs, published 6-8 weeks ahead. These spike hard.
7. **Reviews.** Ask every buyer for a review in the delivery email.

## 6. 30 / 60 / 90 day roadmap

**Days 1-30: Build and launch**
- Lock the 3 characters and write a one-page character sheet each.
- Make 75 pages (3 minis x 25) plus the 5-page sampler.
- Set up Gumroad/Payhip, MailerLite, and the GitHub Pages landing page.
- Create the Pinterest business account; 6-8 boards; post daily.
- Goal: first 10 sales, 100 email subscribers.

**Days 31-60: Find what works**
- Post 1 faceless video a day; double down on the top 2 formats.
- Launch the mega bundle; publish the first KDP paperback.
- Start the email sequence; add the Coloring Club at $7/mo.
- Goal: $500-800/mo, 500 subscribers.

**Days 61-90: Compound**
- Ship a holiday pack ahead of season; add a teacher bundle.
- Publish 2 more KDP books; ask for reviews.
- Goal: $1,000-1,500/mo, 1,500 subscribers.

**Months 4-9:** keep the weekly rhythm, build the club, and add a second
character line. $4k/month typically needs the club and a strong Pinterest
library working together.

## 8. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| IP complaints | Original characters only; no brand names in titles or tags |
| AI-art rules (KDP requires AI disclosure; AI images may not be copyrightable) | Follow `AI_ART_PLAYBOOK.md`; heavy human edit; character sheets; disclose when asked |
| Slow growth | Pinterest compounds; track weekly; change formats, not the whole plan |
| Burnout | Batch weekly; evergreen content first |
| Platform dependence | Everything funnels to your email list |
