# Pinterest Launch Kit: 30 Pins

**Setup (free):** Pinterest Business account named "Inky & Me Coloring
Club". Bio: *Free printable coloring pages for toddlers & preschoolers.
Big, bold, easy-to-color. We color together! 🖍️*

**Boards (create all 8):**
1. Free Printable Coloring Pages for Kids
2. Toddler Coloring Pages
3. Preschool Activities Printable
4. Rainy Day Activities for Kids
5. Birthday Party Activities for Kids
6. Beach & Summer Coloring Pages
7. Holiday Coloring Pages for Kids
8. Classroom Printables for Teachers

**Pin design (Canva free, 1000 x 1500):** a coloring page on top, half
colored-in, half blank; a bold title bar; "FREE PRINTABLE" badge; the
`inkyandme` handle at the bottom. Make 3 designs per page by changing the
title, the colors and the crop.

**Link:** every pin goes to your landing page (the sampler signup) unless it
says otherwise.

**Cadence:** 5-10 pins/day, scheduled in Pinterest's own free scheduler.
One batch session per week.
