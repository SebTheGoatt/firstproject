# Faceless Short Video Scripts (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts)

**Gear:** your phone. **Stand:** a stack of books plus a rubber band, or
tape the phone to a cereal box. Film top-down near a window for free light.
**Edit:** CapCut free or each app's built-in editor. **Audio:** each app's
trending sounds (they're licensed inside that app; don't reuse them
elsewhere). **Hands only.** Your 2-year-old's hands coloring are gold, but
never show their face, name or location.

Post the same video to all three platforms. Bio link goes to the landing
page.

---

### 1. Timelapse color-in (your workhorse)
- **Shot:** top-down, blank Inky page; hands color it in; speed it up 8x.
- **On-screen text (0-2s):** "What color should Inky be?"
- **End text:** "Free pages: link in bio 🖍️"
- **Caption:** "Inky can be any color. What would your kid pick? #coloringpages #toddleractivities"

### 2. "Toddler colors vs. me"
- **Shot:** split screen: the same page colored by a toddler (scribbly) and by you (neat).
- **Text:** "Toddler vs. dad: same page" → "Toddler wins. Obviously."
- **Why it works:** funny, relatable, sharable.

### 3. Pack flip-through
- **Shot:** flip through printed pages fast, one per beat.
- **Text:** "25 coloring pages for $4.99" → "Big lines for little hands" → "Link in bio."

### 4. Print → color → certificate
- **Shot:** printer spitting out pages, crayons dumped out, the finished certificate held up.
- **Text:** "Rainy day plan: done in 2 minutes."

### 5. "POV: you need 20 minutes of quiet"
- **Shot:** a toddler's hands coloring (no face), a coffee cup in the corner.
- **Text:** "POV: you need 20 minutes to drink your coffee hot ☕"

### 6. Before / after reveal
- **Shot:** a blank page; jump-cut to a wild rainbow Inky.
- **Text:** "Asked my kid to color Inky. This happened."

### 7. "Draw yourself next to Inky"
- **Shot:** the "Inky & Me" page; a child's hand draws a stick-figure self.
- **Text:** "This page made my kid's whole day." → "It's in the free pack."

### 8. Making-of (the creator angle)
- **Shot:** screen recording: the rough page → cleanup → finished PDF.
- **Text:** "How I make coloring pages for my son" → "Free pack in bio."
- **Why:** builds trust and attracts other parents.

### 9. Count-along
- **Shot:** the balloon page; a finger counts "1… 2… 3!" as each balloon gets colored.
- **Text:** "Counting practice that doesn't feel like practice."

### 10. Seasonal tease (6 weeks before a holiday)
- **Shot:** a flip-through of the holiday pack.
- **Text:** "Inky's Halloween pack: not scary, just silly 🎃"

### 11. "3 rainy-day hacks for toddlers"
- **Shot:** three quick clips: blanket fort, coloring, dance party.
- **Text:** number each hack; coloring is #2 with your page.
- **Why:** value-first content gets saved and shared.

### 12. ASMR crayon sounds
- **Shot:** close-up of crayon on paper, original sound, no music.
- **Text:** "Crayon sounds + Inky. That's it. That's the video."

### 13. Teacher angle
- **Shot:** a stack of pages fanned out, a clipboard.
- **Text:** "Teachers: 150+ printable pages for early finishers" → "Classroom license in bio."

### 14. Pack-the-bag
- **Shot:** pages folded into a diaper bag with a 4-pack of crayons.
- **Text:** "Restaurant survival kit: $0.10 a page."

### 15. Milestone / thank-you
- **Shot:** a printed "thank you" page; colored certificates spread out.
- **Text:** "100 families are coloring with Inky. Thank you! 🖍️"
