    Common questions

    Straight answers. The full details are in our terms and refund policy.

    What day do you mow my area?

    Each area has its own day, so the crew stays in one place all day: Spring on Mondays, Klein on Tuesdays, New Caney and Splendora on Wednesdays, Colony Ridge on Thursdays, and Cleveland and Plum Grove on Fridays. Saturday is kept for make-up visits.

    How much does a mow cost?

    A flat price per visit, set by your lawn size and yard type. You see the full price with tax before you book. If 2 or more neighbors within half a mile are already booked, you get 5% off the mowing (10% with 5 or more).

    Do I have to sign a contract?

    No. There is no contract and no subscription. Book one visit at a time, whenever you need it.

    How do I pay?

    Pay the crew in cash at the visit, or by card through a private Stripe link we text you. Tips are never expected.

    What happens if it rains?

    We don't mow in lightning, heavy rain or on ground soft enough to rut. We move your visit to a make-up day and text you. If the new day doesn't work for you, you get a full refund.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Cancel any time before the crew arrives: a full refund if you paid by card, nothing owed if you planned to pay cash.

    What if I'm not happy with the work?

    Tell us within 48 hours, with a photo if you can. We come back and fix it at no charge. If it still isn't right, you get a full refund for that visit.

    Do you mow outside these areas?

    Not yet. We only book addresses on our routes, so the crew isn't driving across town. Check your address at the top of the page to see if you're covered.
