      Free tools

      no account needed
      • SquooshShrink job photos before you post them, so they load fast.
      • OpenCutEdit the Friday TikTok or Reel: trim, add text, export.
      • PairDropSend photos and videos from your phone to your computer.
      • Stirling PDFSign, merge and shrink PDFs: insurance papers, the assumed-name form.
      • GristA spreadsheet for mileage, fuel and equipment costs at tax time.
      • ExcalidrawSketch a yard or a sign layout to show the crew or a customer.
      • PexelsFree video clips for ads and Reels, no credit needed. Use grass, blades and stripes, not people.
      • MixkitMore free clips, same rules. Good trimmer and edging close-ups.

      Before posting a yard photo, check that no house number, street sign or car plate shows. Stock clips are filler: never show a stranger as if they were our crew. Your own job photos and videos always win.
