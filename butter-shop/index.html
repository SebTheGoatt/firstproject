01

Squeeze slowly

Press from the middle and let go. A slow press gives the deepest dent and the longest rise.

02

Wipe, don't soak

Clean it with a damp cloth and a drop of mild soap. Let it air dry. Water inside the foam slows the rise.

03

Keep it out of the sun

Heat and UV can yellow and stiffen the foam over time. A drawer is perfect.
