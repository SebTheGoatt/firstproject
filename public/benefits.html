Your Education Benefits

GI Bill Benefits Guide

Everything you need to know about your education benefit options — explained clearly, without the government jargon.

Heads up: Benefit amounts, eligibility rules, and rates change annually. Always verify current figures directly with the VA at va.gov/education/ or by calling 1-888-GIBILL-1. This guide is for educational purposes.
GI Bill Chapters

Which GI Bill Is Right for You?

There are four main GI Bill programs (called "chapters"). Most veterans qualify for Post-9/11 (Chapter 33), but your situation may make another chapter more advantageous.

Chapter 33

Post-9/11 GI Bill (Most Popular)

For veterans who served at least 90 days of active duty after Sept. 10, 2001. The most comprehensive GI Bill available, covering tuition, housing, and books.

  • Up to 100% of in-state public school tuition OR a private school cap (~$26,381/yr)
  • Monthly housing allowance (BAH based on school ZIP code)
  • Up to $1,000/year for books and supplies
  • 36 months of benefits total
  • Transferable to spouse or dependents (with service requirements)
  • Yellow Ribbon Program eligibility at participating schools
Chapter 30

Montgomery GI Bill – Active Duty (MGIB-AD)

For veterans who contributed $100/month for 12 months during service. Pays a flat monthly stipend directly to you — useful if you're at a school where Chapter 33 housing allowance is lower.

  • Monthly payment ($2,300+/month for full-time, 2024 rates)
  • Paid directly to the veteran — you pay the school
  • 36 months of benefits
  • Can be combined with tuition assistance in some cases
  • No housing allowance (the monthly stipend is all-in)
Chapter 35

Survivors' & Dependents' Educational Assistance (DEA)

For spouses and children of veterans who are permanently and totally disabled due to a service-related condition, or who died in service.

  • Monthly stipend for education and training
  • Up to 45 months of education benefits
  • Covers college, vocational/technical training, apprenticeships
  • Eligible dependents: spouses and children of qualifying veterans
  • Can be used concurrently with other VA benefits in some cases
Chapter 1606

Montgomery GI Bill – Selected Reserve (MGIB-SR)

For members of the Selected Reserve (National Guard or Reserves) who have a 6-year service obligation.

  • Monthly stipend (lower rate than Chapter 30)
  • 36 months of benefits
  • Available while still in the Reserves/Guard
  • Can be suspended if called to active duty
  • Eligibility ends when Reserve obligation ends
Post-9/11 Eligibility

Chapter 33 Benefit Tiers

Your Chapter 33 benefit level depends on how long you served on active duty after Sept. 10, 2001.

Service Requirement Benefit Level Tuition Coverage Housing Allowance
36+ months active duty 100% Full (up to cap) Full BAH (E-5 with dependents)
30 months active duty 90% 90% of cap 90% of BAH
24 months active duty 80% 80% of cap 80% of BAH
18 months active duty 70% 70% of cap 70% of BAH
12 months active duty 60% 60% of cap 60% of BAH
90 days active duty 40% 40% of cap 40% of BAH
Understanding Your Pay

What You'll Actually Receive

Chapter 33 benefits come in three parts — tuition, housing (BAH), and the book stipend. Here's how each one works:

🏫 Tuition & Fees

Paid directly to your school. Covers up to 100% of in-state public tuition or ~$26,381/year at private schools (2024). You never see this money — it goes school-to-school.

🏠 Monthly Housing Allowance (BAH)

Paid directly to you, monthly. Based on the E-5 with dependents BAH rate for your school's ZIP code. Online-only students receive a flat rate (~$1,037/month, 2024). Must be enrolled more than half-time to receive it.

📚 Books & Supplies Stipend

Up to $1,000/academic year ($500/semester). Paid directly to you at the start of each term. Pro-rated based on your enrollment (credit hours).

Pro tip: Use the official VA GI Bill Comparison Tool at va.gov/gi-bill-comparison-tool to get exact BAH rates for any school.
Yellow Ribbon Program

Covering the Gap at Private Schools

If you're attending a private school that costs more than the Chapter 33 cap, the Yellow Ribbon Program can cover the difference — at no cost to you.

  • The school contributes a set amount above the Chapter 33 cap
  • The VA matches the school's contribution dollar-for-dollar
  • Together they can cover 100% of tuition at participating schools
  • Only available to 100% benefit-tier veterans
  • Each school sets how many students can participate per year
Example:

Private school tuition: $40,000/year
Chapter 33 cap: $26,381
Gap: $13,619
School Yellow Ribbon contribution: $6,809
VA match: $6,810
Your out-of-pocket: $0

Choosing a School

What to Look for in a VA-Approved School

✅

VA Approval

Every school that accepts GI Bill benefits must be VA-approved. Check the VA WEAM (Web Enabled Approval Management System) or use the GI Bill Comparison Tool to verify.

🎉

Yellow Ribbon Participation

If you're considering a private school, check if they participate in the Yellow Ribbon Program and how many slots they offer — slots can run out each academic year.

⚠️

Watch Out for Predatory Schools

Some for-profit schools specifically target veterans' benefits. Check graduation rates, loan default rates, and employer acceptance before enrolling. Ask: will employers recognize this degree?

🎓

Student Veterans of America Chapters

Schools with SVA chapters tend to have better support systems for veteran students — peer mentorship, priority registration, dedicated advising, and community.

📅

Enrollment Certification Timeline

Your school's certifying official must submit your enrollment to the VA. Find out their timeline early — late certification means delayed BAH payments.

💰

In-State Tuition Rights

Under the Veterans Choice Act, veterans using Chapter 33 or 30 are entitled to in-state tuition rates at public schools regardless of how long they've lived in the state.

Getting Started

How to Apply for GI Bill Benefits

The application process is straightforward — it just takes a few steps.

01
Apply Online

Submit VA Form 22-1990 at va.gov/education/apply-for-education-benefits/

02
Get Your COE

Receive your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) — typically takes 30 days.

03
Enroll in School

Provide your COE to your school's VA certifying official when you enroll.

04
Benefits Activate

Once your school certifies your enrollment, tuition is paid and BAH begins.
