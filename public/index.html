How the AI Tutor Works

AI That Teaches, Not Completes

The VetPath AI Tutor is built on Claude AI and designed around one principle: you learn more when you do the work. The tutor explains, coaches, and guides — but the thinking stays yours.

  • 1
    Ask a question or share your work

    Type a concept you're struggling with, paste a draft essay, or ask for a study plan.

  • 2
    Get an explanation, not an answer

    The tutor breaks down concepts, asks guiding questions, and points you toward understanding — it won't just hand you a finished product.

  • 3
    Apply it yourself and grow

    Use what you've learned, check your understanding, and ask follow-ups until the material clicks. Your degree means something — make it count.
